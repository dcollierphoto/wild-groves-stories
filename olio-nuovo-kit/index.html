Your Security Team (Polyphenols)

Cartoon · 1 min 14 sec · Instagram Reel + Facebook

Meet your security team. 🫒

Olive trees make natural compounds called polyphenols to protect their fruit from bugs, sun and disease. In olive oil, they help protect the fats in your blood from oxidation.

The fresher the oil, the bigger the team. That's why we wait all year for Olio Nuovo, the first oil of the harvest.

Now at the Wild Groves tasting room in Newcastle, CA, and at wildgroves.com.

Ancient Rome used so much olive oil that the empty jars built a hill. 🏺

Monte Testaccio is still there today: a mountain of broken olive oil jars, about 2,000 years old.
