First Prize

Cartoon · 56 sec · Instagram Reel + Facebook

At the ancient Olympics, the winner got a crown of olive leaves. That's it. Leaves. 🌿

But in Athens, they had a better idea. Win there, and they gave you the oil: ten gallons a jar. Win the chariot race, and you went home with 140 jars. 🏺

2,500 years later, first prize is still olive oil. You don't have to win a chariot race for Olio Nuovo, but you do have to be quick.

At the Wild Groves tasting room in Newcastle, CA, and at wildgroves.com.
