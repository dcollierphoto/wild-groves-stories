How to add it

  1. In Shopify, go to Online Store → Blog posts → Add blog post.
  2. Paste the Title below into the title box.
  3. In the content box, click the <> button (Show HTML). Click Copy article below and paste it in. Click <> again to go back. The photos and headings will appear.
  4. Under Featured image, upload the photo below (download it first).
  5. Paste the Excerpt into the excerpt box, and the Search description under "Search engine listing."
  6. Set the author, then save. Set it to Visible when you're ready to publish.
